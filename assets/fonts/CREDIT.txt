  Font "Alagard" by Hewett Tsoi — freeware (https://www.dafont.com/alagard.font).
  Sprite-font atlas by Ramon Santamaria (@raysan5), shipped with raylib as
  examples/text/resources/sprite_fonts/alagard.png.

  It is a raylib IMAGE FONT: 95 glyphs (ASCII 32..126) in 16px bands, separated by a
  magenta (255,0,255) key colour. LoadFont() on a .png feeds it straight into
  LoadFontFromImage(image, MAGENTA, 32) — no .fnt sidecar, no metrics file.

  The glyphs are pure white, so DrawTextEx's tint multiply gives the colour asked for.
  Do not recolour the atlas.
